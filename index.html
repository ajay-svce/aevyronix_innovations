Building materials and technologies for a sustainable future

Can materials that are treated as waste today become valuable resources tomorrow?

Aevyronix Innovations is a technology-development company working across biomaterials, sustainable technology and biotechnology.

Silk processing produces large volumes of protein-rich wastewater and by-products. Our first research programme focuses on sericin, the natural silk protein in those streams. We recover, process, purify, hydrolyze and characterize it, then convert it into useful biomaterial products.

The aim is not to add one more ingredient to the market. We are building consistent, scalable, application-focused materials that industries and researchers can use as raw materials.
